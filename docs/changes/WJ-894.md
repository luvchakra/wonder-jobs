# WJ-894 · Jobs · DONE

**What:** The three limits of WJ-891 (a role with its own résumé) are fixed:
- **Ranking:** searching as such a role ranks jobs with the file's skills (less those taken off, plus those added by hand), not the role's stored copy.
- **Removing:** taking one of the file's skills off in the role's form sticks.
- **Adding:** a skill added by hand counts as hand-added even when My profile also has it. It's recorded as it's added, not guessed by comparing with My profile.

**Why:** Owner, 2026-11-28: "yes, to all recommendations" (the limits listed on #818).

**Notes:**
- `CareerRole` gains `skillsOwn` (its `skills` are exactly those added or changed by hand) and `skillsRemoved` (the file's skills taken off, at most 60).
- `domain/career/roleFile.ts`: `fileRoleSkills` reads them. `editFileRoleSkills` records an edit from the form. Adding a removed skill back clears its removal.
- Roles saved before this (`skillsOwn` unset) keep WJ-891's rule until their form is saved.
- Ranking: `lib/roleFileCache.ts` keeps the files read this session (at most 8) so scoring can use them synchronously. The jobs store's `scoreJob` and the run's `runProfile` use `roleWithFileSkills`. A run searching as such a role reads its file before matching. Find reads the roles' files once on open and rescores.
- Tests in `roleFile.test.ts` cover all three: a hand-added My-profile skill is kept, a removal sticks and adding back undoes it, and ranking uses the file's skills once read.
- Checked at 390px with a saved résumé renamed to Deepak as the role's résumé: removing "Claims" and adding "Product Strategy" (a My profile skill) both hold after Save changes and a reload. No sideways scroll.
- No automation, AI or external-action change.
