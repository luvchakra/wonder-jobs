# WJ-829: React, comment and reshare on network posts; post a listing from its card; New message from the +

- **What:**
  - **Under every feed post or update:** Like (a reaction), Comment, and (on a post for everyone on the network) Reshare.
    - Like opens Like, Celebrate, Support and Insightful, one per member per post. Pressing your own reaction again takes it back.
    - Comments are shown to whoever sees the post. A member deletes their own comments, and any comment on their own post. Report on a comment hides it for the reporter at once and for everyone at three reporters, the same as posts.
    - Reshare is a new post with the member's own words (or none) that quotes the original. Resharing a reshare quotes the original. A post for connections can't be reshared, so it never travels further than its author chose. If the original is deleted, the reshare says it's no longer available.
    - Counts show (reactions with their top kinds, comments, reshares). The author is told about a comment or a reshare.
    - Delete and Report moved under a ⋯ on the post.
  - **Post to my network on listing cards** (owner: "add option here to post this to network with custom message on top"). A speech-bubble button before Share on every module's result card and on Jobs' card opens the post sheet with that listing attached. Shown only when signed in and the listing has a public page.
  - **New message** (owner: "clicking + here should give an option to send message to a person"). On Messages, the + asks between New message and Post to your network. From 768px, New message sits at the top of Messages. You pick one of your connections and their conversation opens.
- **Why:** Owner requests, 2026-11-21.
- **Notes:**
  - Migration `0055_network_reactions_comments.sql`: `network_reactions` (one row per post and member), `network_comments`, and `network_updates.reshare_of`, each with indexes. Until it is applied, the feed shows no counts and these actions fail with a message; posting is unaffected.
  - Server code is in `server/network/engage.ts`. Visibility goes through `social.ts::seeable`: the member's own post, a connection's, or a post for everyone whose author is findable by everyone. Never a blocked person's, and never one the member reported or that three members reported. Routes are under `api/network/updates/[id]`.
  - **Security / governance:**
    - Reacting, commenting and resharing go through `resolveCapability("network.post")`; its description now names them. The press is the confirmation, as for posts.
    - Comments and reshares are audited without their words (`COMMENT_POSTED`, `POST_PUBLISHED`).
    - Commenters leave the server only as a `MemberView`, tiered for each viewer, and never with an account id.
    - Account export includes the member's comments and reactions. Erasure and leaving the network remove theirs, plus every reaction and comment on their posts (`network.ts::dropUpdates`). Deleting a post removes its reactions and comments.
  - Tests: `server/network/engage.test.ts` covers visibility, reaction changes, comment delete rights, blocks, report thresholds, reshare rules and quoting, deletion cascade, export and erasure.
