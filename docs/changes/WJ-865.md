# WJ-865: Community moderation like Reddit: moderators with permissions, AutoMod, adding members

- **What:**
  - **Add members:** the owner, and moderators who manage members, invite their own connections from Members → Add members. Each invitee is told and joins with one tap (even in an approval-only community) or declines. Invitations show under Invited and can be withdrawn.
  - **Moderators with permissions:** Everything, Members, Posts & replies, Settings (`domain/network/communities.ts::hasPerm`).
    - The owner, or a moderator with Everything, invites a member to moderate. They become a moderator once they accept, and can later be given other permissions, removed, or step down themselves.
    - Moderators from before this keep full powers.
    - Only an owner deletes the community or makes someone owner.
  - **Bans for a time:** 1, 7 or 30 days or permanent, with a reason the person is told. A timed ban ends by itself. Banned people are listed, with Lift ban.
  - **Mod tab (for moderators):**
    - **Queue:** held and reported posts, with why. Approve, or Remove with a reason (one of the community's rules, or the moderator's own words) that the author is told.
    - **Log:** every moderator's and AutoMod's action.
    - **AutoMod:** its rules.
  - **Removal keeps the post:** a moderator's removal no longer deletes the post. It's kept for its author (marked with why) and the moderators; others see "Removed by the moderators" in its place.
  - **AutoMod, in every community, on by default:** it is listed with the moderators and checks each new discussion and reply (moderators' own pass, `autoModCheck`). It:
    - holds, or removes, posts with watched words;
    - holds web links and more than 3 posts a day from members who joined in the last 2 days;
    - holds anything 3 members report.
    Moderators with Settings change all of it.
- **Why:** Owner requests (2026-11-23): "add option to add moderators in the community, moderators will have multiple functions, make it similar to reddit. also have some automatic mods in each community, similar to reddit", and "give option to owner and mod to add members".
- **Notes:**
  - Migration 0061 adds member columns (`perms`, `mod_invite`, `invited_by`, `banned_until`), `network_communities.automod`, `state` / `mod_note` on discussions and replies, and two tables, `network_community_reports` and `network_mod_log`, each indexed by community and person.
  - Until it's applied, new posts AutoMod would hold, and the new moderator actions, say "Communities aren't switched on yet."
- **Security and governance:**
  - Every permission is checked on the server against the caller's own membership.
  - Invitations go only to the inviter's connections, through `resolveCapability("network.connect")`, and nobody joins without saying yes.
  - AutoMod is plain rules, with no model: it holds or removes, and never bans.
  - Moderator actions are audited and logged.
  - Account erasure removes a person's reports, turns them into "former member" in the mod log, and clears invitations they sent. Export includes reports filed and moderator actions taken (`erasure.coverage.test.ts` entries added).
