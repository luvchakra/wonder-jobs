# WJ-827: Deleting an account keeps the hiring workspaces the person owns

- **What:**
  - Deleting an account no longer deletes a hiring workspace that person created or owns. The workspace, its jobs, applications, conversations and notes are the company's records and stay.
  - When the person was the only owner, the longest-standing remaining member becomes owner. With nobody left, the workspace stays and its open jobs and listings close, so no one applies into a workspace nobody reads.
  - What was the person's goes: their membership (the tenant cascade), their account id on the company's records (workspace, jobs, careers site, invitations, shortlist, conversations, company messages, reviews, module listings), handed to the next owner or marked `former-member`; their name on reviews ("Former team member"); a listing's "show my profile" choices.
  - Export includes the person's workspace memberships (company, role, joined).
- **Why:** Owner decision (2026-11-21): "no" to "Decide whether deleting an account should also delete a hiring workspace that person owns". Until now `hiring_orgs.created_by` cascaded, so deleting the creator's account deleted the whole workspace.
- **Notes:**
  - `server/hiringAccount.ts` (`exportHiring`, `eraseHiring`), registered in `server/privacy/accountData.ts` as `hiring`; it runs before the tenant row is deleted.
  - Migration `0054_hiring_workspace_survives_creator.sql`: `hiring_orgs.created_by` becomes nullable, `on delete set null`. Until it is applied, an erasure that can't empty `created_by` stops with "apply migration 0054" rather than lose the workspace.
  - **Security / governance:** no new cross-tenant read. Records change hands only within their own workspace (`org_id` filter on every update). A former member's account id leaves every hiring row.
  - Tests: `server/hiringAccount.test.ts` (sole owner leaves → promotion and hand-over; another owner stays; nobody left → jobs close; a non-owner leaves; runs twice safely).
