# WJ-880 · Jobs · DONE

**What:** After a search for words or as a role, an AI relevance check reads the 40 leading results against what was searched and rates how relevant each is (0–100), with a one-line reason.
- A result it rates unrelated (under 25) drops to Low fit and sinks.
- Others move toward its rating by half the gap, at most +15 / −25.
- The job shows the reason as "AI relevance check (AI-generated)", and the search's evidence shows "Checked by AI" with how many results and how long it took.
- Nothing is hidden. Automation → "Check search results with AI" (on by default) turns it off.

**Why:** Owner, 2026-11-25: "can you use ai to do the matching and finding the right results", then "go ahead with both steps" (step 1 was WJ-559, #803).

**Notes:**
- **Governance:**
  - New capability `ai_match` (low risk, not external, default Automatic), resolved through `resolveCapability` via the run's `ctx.policy`. "Ask me" can't be answered mid-search, so it doesn't run; "Off" doesn't run. No model, an unusable reply or any error also leaves the rules' ranking as it is (fails closed).
  - The model is reached only through `services/ai/service.ts` (`AIService.matchJobs`): WonderWorks AI or the candidate's own key.
  - The new prompt id `match_jobs` lives in `server/ai/systemPrompts.ts` and ends with SYSTEM_GUARD. The search and postings go in as tagged data (tag look-alikes stripped), never as instructions. Its JSON reply is exempt from the instruction-echo check, like `career_history`.
  - The reply is a proposal. `domain/jobs/aiMatch.ts` keeps only ids it sent, clamps scores and trims reasons; `applyAiRelevance` decides the change within fixed limits.
- **Cost:** one call per search, on 40 postings, up to 3,000 output tokens. The token cost is recorded in AI usage like other calls, and the search shows the time taken. Reads are cached per job and per search words (`aiRelevance` in the jobs store, persisted only for the jobs kept, like `aiFits`). On WonderWorks AI, each call counts against the plan's monthly AI drafts (`/api/ai/complete`); the shared-layer session is asking the owner whether to exempt it.
- **Tests:** `aiMatch.test.ts` (parse, merge, data tags) and `aiRelevance.test.ts` (run / ask / off / failure / no model, and the default).
- **Other:** records Jobs' new id block WJ-880–899 in CLAUDE.md, at the shared-layer session's request. Supersedes WJ-559's note that a second AI pass wasn't needed: the owner asked for it.
