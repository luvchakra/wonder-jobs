# WJ-786 · WorksLake companies · DONE

## Logos for organisations known only by a name written another way

**What:** `resolveCompanyByName` looks up an organisation that's known only by its name on Wikidata, so its logo can be fetched. It now also tries the other ways listings tend to write that name:
- **An acronym written into the name:** "Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Madras" is also tried as "Indian Institute of Technology Madras" and as "IIT Madras".
- **A government body written with its government:** "Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India" is also tried as "Ministry of Tribal Affairs".

It also checks the top three results instead of only the first. A hit counts when its label or the alias it matched is exactly that name, it isn't a person, and it has an official website.

**Why:** Owner, 2026-11-07: "iit madras has a well defined logo, why can't you get and show it, fix the logo issues". Written the listing's way, the name matched nothing on Wikidata, so no domain was found and the tile showed "I".

**Checked live** (the unit tests stay offline):
- IIT Madras → iitm.ac.in (Q858662).
- Ministry of Tribal Affairs → tribal.nic.in (Q6867635).

Each name costs at most three searches, once; after that it's cached in the company directory as before.
