# WJ-818: Network opens on a feed with posts; People, Invitations and Connections become icons; the title moves to the top bar

- **What:**
  - **Feed:** Network now opens on a feed in place of the Updates tab. It shows what the member's connections post and do (new role, status turned on, shared listing), the member's own, and posts their authors addressed to everyone on the network.
  - **Posts:** a box at the top ("Share something with your network") posts up to 1,500 characters to My connections or to Everyone on the network. The author can Delete their own. Anyone else can Report a post.
  - **Icons:** People, Invitations (with its count), Connections, Messages and My profile are a row of icons above the feed. Pressing the open one again goes back to the feed. The tabs are gone.
  - **Title:** below 1024px the top bar reads "My network" on Network's pages, in place of the module menu. From 1024px the page keeps its own title.
- **Why:** Owner request (2026-11-21), marked up on a screenshot: "move people, invitations and connections add icons above, move my network to top navbar, remove updates tab, instead show a feed of what other people are doing and posting on network".
- **Notes:**
  - Migration `0052_network_feed.sql`: one index on `network_updates (kind, created_at desc)` for reading posts addressed to everyone. No new column: a post is a `network_updates` row of kind `post` or `post:everyone`. The feed works without the index, just unindexed.
  - **Security / governance:**
    - Posting goes through `resolveCapability("network.post")` (new, default automatic; the box shows what goes and to whom) and is audited as `POST_PUBLISHED`, without the words. At most 20 posts a day.
    - A non-connection's post shows only if its author is findable by everyone. Blocked people's posts never show.
    - A member can delete only their own rows (the delete is filtered by tenant).
    - A report (shared `module_reports`, module `network`) hides the post from its reporter at once, and from everyone at three different reporters. The author isn't told.
    - Posts are part of `network_updates`, so account deletion and export already cover them (WJ-815).
  - Tests: `server/network/social.test.ts` (audiences, delete, report, block), `domain/network/policy.test.ts`, the route journeys test (the feed now includes the member's own items, marked `mine`), and `e2e/network.spec.ts` (icons in place of tabs; all 6 pass locally on chromium).
  - Not built: likes, comments, photos, or an admin screen for reported posts. Reports are stored for the existing moderation work to pick up.
