# WJ-615 · Co-founders · DONE

**What:** Deleting an account now removes the person's Co-founders data, and "Download my data" includes it. Co-founders' entry (`cofounders`) is added to `server/privacy/accountData.ts`; the code is `server/cofounders/account.ts`.
**Why:** The seven Co-founders tables (migration 0040) have no foreign key to `tenants`, so deleting the tenant row left a deleted person's profile, connections, messages, blocks, reports and team memberships behind. Requested by the shared-layer session (E2E and security run, 2026-11-21); the owner said yes.
**Notes:** Erase removes the profile, every connection the person is on or started (pair connections, join requests, invitations they sent) with its messages, the messages they sent elsewhere, their blocks and the blocks that name them, their reports and the reports about them. **Teams:** a team they founded alone is closed and removed, with its other members and the join requests on it; a team with another founder carries on without them: only their membership goes, their approvals, build marks and shared email come off its join requests, and if they created it another founder becomes its creator. The profile is removed last, so a run that stopped part-way finishes on the next try; running it twice, or for a person with no Co-founders data, is a no-op. Export holds only the person's own data: their profile, the teams they are on (their role and a head count, never who), their connections, their sent messages and shared email, counts of blocks and the reports they filed; never another person's account id, name, hidden words or contact, and never join requests other people made to their team. Tests: `server/cofounders/account.test.ts` (no row still names the deleted tenant, the team rules, another person's data survives, export contents, twice and empty). Help updated (Co-founders → Your data). No migration; tenant filters are on every query (the person's own tenant, or a team after reading their own membership).
