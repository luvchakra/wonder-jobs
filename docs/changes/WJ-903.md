# WJ-903: Launch gift (first month of Max free) and the launch splash

- **What:**
  - **Launch gift:** every account that pays for nothing has Max free for its first 30 days from sign-up. Accounts made before the gift began (2026-11-28) count from then. No card, nothing to cancel; afterwards Free unless the member chooses a plan. A paid subscription, and an admin's testing plan, always win.
    - `domain/billing/plans.ts`: `LaunchGift` in the plans config (on, plan, days, since; defaults on/Max/30/2026-11-28), `launchGiftUntil`, `launchGiftLine`; merged field by field like the rest.
    - `server/billing/service.ts::entitlement` applies it (`gift: true`, reason "Launch gift — Max free until …"). The account's start is its `tenants.created_at` (`server/supabase.ts::tenantCreatedAt`, cached; an unknown date counts from the gift's start, never from "now"). A failed read still fails closed to Free.
    - Admin → Billing → Plans & features: a Launch gift card (on/off, plan, days), saved and audited with the plans (`launchGift.*` in the diff).
    - Plan & billing (in the app): a line naming the gift and its end date, and both paid plans offered as "Choose …" to continue after it. Checkout itself is unchanged (it reads only what's paid for).
    - Landing: the quiet page has one line under Get started; the full page's pricing names the gift and features Max while the gift is on.
  - **Launch splash:** opened as the installed app (home screen, display-mode standalone), the mark grows in with a soft glow and "WonderWorks" rises under it, then the screen fades out: about 1.3 s, once per launch (session). An inline script marks `<html data-splash>` before paint, so the app never flashes first, and no React is involved. Reduced motion: a still mark for half a second. Never shown in a browser tab.
- **Why:** Owner (2026-11-28): "let's not make it payable for first one month as a gift for users as a launch gift. mention this in landing page as well as in payment section in app. keep default plan as maximum plan"; "show a nice splash animation when the app opens up from the home page of the device".
- **Notes:** The gift is on by default, so production accounts have Max until 2026-12-28 (older accounts) or a month from sign-up. Governance: no gate changes; plan limits read the entitlement as before. Help updated (Plan & billing). Tests: `plans.test.ts` (dates, merge, line), `billing.test.ts` (entitlement with the gift on and over); the billing tests about payments switch the gift off.
- **PR:** see the squash commit.
