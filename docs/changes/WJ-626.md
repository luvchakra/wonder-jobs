# WJ-626 · Buy, Sell & Rent, Contact · DONE

**What:** In a Buy, Sell & Rent conversation, "Share my email" is replaced by the shared Contact details card: you choose a phone and/or email to share; the other person sees it masked until they share theirs, then you both see each other's in full. Stop sharing takes yours back at once; blocking takes back what both shared. The "View network profile" link now appears once both have shared.
**Why:** Owner (2026-11-21), via the shared-layer session: phone and email "masked until both agree", built once in WonderWorks and used everywhere (WJ-831 core).
**Notes:** Privacy and external effect. Uses `server/contact/exchange.ts` (rows encrypted at rest, in `ACCOUNT_DATA` as `contact`); this module's own check is `stand` (the thread's buyer or seller), the gate stays `marketplace.share_contact` (description updated; the sheet is the confirmation, audited as `CONTACT_SHARED`/`CONTACT_WITHDRAWN` without the details, the other side's notice carries none). Sharing is refused on an ended conversation and the card is hidden there. The old one-way `shared` email on a thread is no longer written or shown (the column stays; people re-share from the card, and old rows go with their thread on deletion); I did not migrate old emails into offers, since each was shared in full by a press that promised no masking, and re-asking is the safer default. Tests in `marketplace.test.ts` (masked until both, withdraw, gate ask/off, outsiders 404, ended thread, block takes back, network link only after both) and `policy.test.ts`. Needs migration 0057 applied (until then the card doesn't show).
