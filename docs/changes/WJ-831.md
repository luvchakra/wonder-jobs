# WJ-831: Share phone and email safely, masked until both agree

- **What:** A shared contact exchange in the WonderWorks layer, used first in Network conversations.
  - At the top of a connection's conversation, **Contact details** → **Share my phone or email** opens a sheet. You tick phone, email or both; they are prefilled from your Career Profile and can be edited.
  - The other person sees your details **masked** (`+9198•••••210`, `ku•••@gmail.com`) until they share theirs. Once both have shared, each sees the other's in full, with tap-to-call and tap-to-email.
  - **Change** edits what you share. **Stop sharing** deletes your offer at once, and the other person is back to masked (or nothing).
- **Why:** Owner request (2026-11-21): "add a feature to safely share phone number and email with masking option on by default and share only on mutual consent". The owner chose:
  - masked until both agree;
  - built once in the shared layer, for Network, Co-founders and Buy, Sell & Rent.
- **Notes:**
  - **Rules:** `domain/contact/contact.ts` covers masking and checking phone numbers and emails.
  - **Storage:** `server/contact/exchange.ts`.
    - Table `contact_exchanges` (migration `0057`) holds one row per person per conversation (module + subject), encrypted.
    - The module checks who may take part; the core decides what each side sees.
    - Until 0057 is applied, sharing says it isn't switched on yet.
  - **Screens:** `wonderworks/contact/ContactExchange.tsx`.
  - **Network:** `server/network/contact.ts`, route `api/network/threads/[id]/contact` (GET, POST, DELETE).
  - **Co-founders and Buy, Sell & Rent** keep their one-way email share until their sessions move onto this core (briefed).
  - **Security / governance:**
    - New capability `network.share_contact`: medium risk, external, default **Ask**, gated through `resolveCapability`. The sheet is the confirmation.
    - Audited `CONTACT_SHARED` / `CONTACT_WITHDRAWN`, without the details.
    - Phone and email are AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest, bound to the module, conversation and person. They are decrypted only for the owner, or for the other person once both have shared.
    - Available only on a live, unblocked connection, between its two people; a block ends it.
    - The other person is notified without the details.
  - **Account data:** export includes your offers. Erasure removes your offers and those made to you (`ACCOUNT_DATA` entry `contact`, registered in the erasure coverage test).
  - **Tests:** `server/contact/exchange.test.ts`, `server/network/contact.test.ts`, the policy test, the route journey's 401 list, and the erasure coverage world.
