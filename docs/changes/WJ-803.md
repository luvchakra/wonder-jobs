# WJ-803: Cards: long places stop at three lines

**What.**
- On every module's result cards, each fact under the title (company, place, mode, pay) now stops at three lines.
- A place that lists many countries in brackets is shortened. "Remote (Afghanistan, Albania, Algeria, …)" becomes "Remote (Afghanistan, Albania +148)". Hovering the place still shows the full text.

**Why.** Owner, 2026-11-09: "truncate this after 3 lines". A Part Time Jobs card listed about 150 countries.

**How.**
- `wonderworks/find/parts.tsx::ResultCard` wraps each fact in `line-clamp-3`.
- `wonderworks/find/place.ts::shortPlace` handles a bracketed list longer than three: it keeps the first two items and counts the rest.
