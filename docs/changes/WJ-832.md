# WJ-832: Deleting an account removes its reports and blocks in every module

- **What:** A new `ACCOUNT_DATA` entry, `safety` (`server/modules/safety.ts::eraseSafety` / `exportSafety`). On account deletion it removes, in every module:
  - the reports the person filed;
  - the reports about them;
  - their blocks, both ways.
  Before this, only Network's and Buy, Sell & Rent's own reports went, so a listing report filed in Jobs' or another module's Find outlived the account. Export now lists the reports the person filed and how many people they blocked.
- **Why:** Found by the erasure coverage test (WJ-846, E2E session, 2026-11-21) as a plain miss. The test now expects it gone.
