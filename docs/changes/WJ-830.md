# WJ-830: Photos on posts, Repost like X, a richer listing card, Message under ⋯

- **What:**
  - **Photos on posts** (owner, 2026-11-21: "add option to add images and video here"). New post has a Photo button that adds up to four JPEG, PNG or WebP photos.
    - Each photo is shrunk on the device to at most 2048 px and sent while the member writes. It stays theirs alone until they press Post. Closing the sheet takes the photos back, and any never posted are removed after a day.
    - A post may now be photos alone, with no text. The feed shows one photo full width, or two to four in a grid; tapping one shows it large.
  - **"Couldn't react: Something went wrong"** (owner screenshot). Production doesn't have migration 0055 yet. Until it does, Like, Comment and Reshare now say they aren't switched on yet.
  - **Repost** (owner: "add option to repost the post on right of comment icon, similar to retweet in Twitter/X"). Repost sits to the right of Comment on every post. Its menu offers Repost (to your connections, at once) and Quote (your words on top, with an audience to pick). The button then reads Reposted, and Undo repost deletes the repost. A post its author kept to connections still can't be reposted, and the button says so. Repost replaces the word "Reshare" everywhere.
  - **Message moves under ⋯** (owner: "take message to here"), together with Say congrats, Delete and Report.
  - **A richer listing card** (owner: "instead of showing just one line of linked post, make it richer"). A post's attached listing shows its preview image, title, who it's from, its facts and the start of its description, read from its public page (`server/network/preview.ts`, `GET /api/network/preview?page=`). While it loads, or if the listing is gone, the card shows the one line kept on the post. The New post sheet shows the attached listing the same way, with × to remove it (owner: "make this more descriptive"). New posts from Share or the card button keep the listing's own title rather than the share email's subject.
  - **Video is not included.** It needs a storage decision first (size and cost); see the PR.
- **Why:** Owner requests, 2026-11-21.
- **Notes:**
  - Migration `0056_network_media.sql` adds the `network_media` table (encrypted photos) and `network_updates.media` (the photo ids, in order), so the feed never reads photos. Until it is applied, a post with photos says photos aren't available yet; posts without photos are unaffected.
  - Server code: `server/network/media.ts` (upload, discard, claim, attach) and `engage.ts::readMedia`. Routes: `api/network/media` (POST, multipart) and `api/network/media/[id]` (GET, DELETE).
  - **Security / privacy:**
    - The file type is read from the file's bytes, and EXIF, GPS and other metadata are stripped by the shared photo handling (`server/modules/photo.ts`). Photos are stored AES-256-GCM encrypted, keyed to the member and the photo id.
    - Photos are served only through the app, `private`, never at a public URL, and only to someone who may see the post (`seeable`). A photo not yet posted is served only to its member.
    - A post can carry only the member's own unposted photos.
    - Deleting a post deletes its photos. Account erasure and leaving the network remove all of the member's photos. Export lists a photo count per post.
  - Tests: `server/network/media.test.ts`, `preview.test.ts`, `myRepost` in `engage.test.ts`, plus the route journey's 401 list.
