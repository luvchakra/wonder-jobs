# WJ-851: Discussions: upvote and downvote; "Join the conversation" at the bottom

- **What:**
  - Community discussions and replies have **up and down arrows** with a score (upvotes minus downvotes) instead of Like. Pressing your own vote again takes it back, and Top sorts by score.
  - The reply box under a discussion's post is replaced by a **"+ Join the conversation"** bar floating at the bottom, which opens the reply box in place.
    - Non-members see "Join *community* to take part".
    - A locked discussion's bar says replies are closed.
    - The round + and ⋯ buttons step aside while the bar shows.
- **Why:** Owner requests (2026-11-22): "instead of like, give upvote, downvote feature here", and "remove the crossed section, instead add a join the conversation at the bottom like attached sample image".
- **Notes:**
  - Migration `0060_network_vote_value.sql` adds `network_votes.value` (+1 / -1, default 1, so existing likes count as upvotes). Until it is applied, upvotes still work and a downvote says it isn't switched on yet.
  - `server/network/communities.ts::vote` replaces `like`. The route action is `vote` (value 1, -1 or 0). The domain fields are `score` and `myVote`.
  - Voting goes through `network.post` as liking did.
  - Tests: `server/network/communities.test.ts`.
- **PR:** see the squash commit.
