# WJ-889 · Jobs · DONE

**What:** In Full Time, when a role's or typed search finds fewer than five jobs above a low fit, the results line says so plainly and the low fits fold.
- The line reads, for example, "Only 3 jobs fit “insurance operations” in your sources right now · searched 11 sources", or "No jobs fit … well" when none do.
- The low fits fold under one "Show N more" button at the end of the list, which takes the place of Show more.
- With five or more fits, or with no low fits, the list is unchanged.
- Profile searches, Part Time and a list with a minimum fit set in Refine are unchanged.

**Why:** Owner, 2026-11-26: "Yes, say it and fold". A domain search such as "Insurance operations" filled the list with off-domain low fits instead of saying few jobs fit.

**Notes:**
- `domain/jobs/fewFound.ts::fewFound` / `fewLine`. Every number is real: the count of jobs above a low fit on screen, and the run's own source count (`config.sourceIds`, else the enabled sources), the same count as the "Searched N sources" line, which opens the run's per-source details.
- The count comes first so a phone's one-line results line still shows it when cut short. The whole line is its title.
- Unfolding lasts for this search: a new search folds again.
- Test: `fewFound.test.ts`.
- Checked at 390px with the sample catalog set to two fits: the line reads "Only 2 jobs fit “actuarial reserving” in your sources right now · searched 14 sources", 2 cards show, "Show 270 more" brings the rest, and nothing scrolls sideways.
- No automation, AI or external-action change.
