# WJ-790 · Email · DONE

## Emails as quiet as the landing page, and readable in dark mode

**What:** every WonderWorks email goes through `server/email/layout.ts`, which now draws a simpler frame:
- **Top:** the WonderWorks mark (a small colour icon) and the name **as live text**: "Wonder" in ink, "Works" in brand purple.
  - A mail app in dark mode recolours text but not pictures, so the name stays readable.
  - The old wordmark PNG's dark "Wonder" disappeared on Gmail's dark background.
- **Body:** the email's content in one plain column on white, up to 560px wide. The coloured top bar, the card borders and the grey page background are gone.
- **Footer:** a thin line, then why the person got the email, then the links (notification settings, privacy, unsubscribe).
- `color-scheme` now declares light and dark.
- Company-branded emails (career-site applications) keep the company's name at the top.

**Why:** Owner, 2026-11-07, on a contact-form email shown in Gmail's dark mode: "can't see the text, fix it, review the email format and make it simple like landing page".
