# WJ-625 · Buy, Sell & Rent, Privacy · DONE

**What:** "Delete my account" now also removes a person's Buy, Sell & Rent listings with their photos, every conversation they are on (with its messages and offers; the other person loses it too) and the blocks and reports that name them. "Download my data" now includes their listings, the conversations they are on and what they sent in them.
**Why:** Shared-layer request (2026-11-21), from the E2E and security run: migration 0042's tables have no foreign key to the tenant row, so deleting the account cascaded to none of them.
**Notes:** Privacy and data rights. Uses the mechanism from WJ-815 (`server/privacy/accountData.ts`, one entry added: key `marketplace`; `server/marketplace/account.ts`). Erase touches only `marketplace` rows in the shared `module_blocks`/`module_reports` tables, is safe to run twice, and is a no-op for a person with no data. The export holds only the person's own data: no other person's account id, name, contact details or words, and offers only where the person is the buyer. Tests in `server/marketplace/account.test.ts` (export contents, full erasure through `eraseAccount`, other people's and other modules' rows surviving, repeat and empty erase). Photos are counted, not exported (the files stay private to the listing).
