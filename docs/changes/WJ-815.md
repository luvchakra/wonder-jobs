# WJ-815: Deleting an account removes its Network data; the export includes it

- **What:**
  - "Delete my account" now removes the member's network profile, their updates, every connection and request they are on with its whole conversation, and the blocks and reports that name them.
  - "Download my data" now includes their network profile, connections, the messages they sent and what they shared.
  - `server/privacy/accountData.ts` is the list of such data; `subjectRights.ts` runs every entry on export and on erasure.
- **Why:** Found while following up the E2E and security run (2026-11-21). The network tables have no foreign key to `tenants`, so deleting the tenant row left them behind: a deleted account's network profile would have stayed visible.
- **Notes:**
  - **Security / privacy:** erasure of this data runs first and stops the whole erasure if it fails, so a retry finishes it. The other person in a conversation loses that conversation. The export carries no other member's account id or words.
  - **Same gap elsewhere, not fixed here:** Co-founders' tables (`0040`) and Buy, Sell & Rent's (`0042`) have no foreign key to `tenants` either. Their sessions are asked to add their entries to `accountData.ts`. CLAUDE.md now states the rule.
  - Tests: `server/network/account.test.ts`.
