# WJ-857: One up-arrow button for the quick actions on phones

- **What:**
  - On a phone, the bottom right shows one round up-arrow button instead of three buttons, in every module and in network mode.
  - Pressed, the actions rise from it one after another, each with its name: + (New post or Create), Network (in network mode, the way back to the module), and ⋯ (the account menu). The arrow turns to point down.
  - They fold away again when the arrow is pressed again, on a tap elsewhere, on Escape, on going to another page, or after 6 seconds left alone. Choosing an action folds them too.
  - Folded actions are `inert` and hidden from screen readers.
- **Why:** Owner request (2026-11-22): "too many icons now, to keep it simple, by default just show one up arrow kind of icon, on click open up these icons in animated way, also wrap up the icons again if not used, have these behaviour in all modules".
- **Notes:**
  - Files: `wonderworks/chrome/MobileNav.tsx`, and `LeaveNetwork.tsx` (now `useLeaveNetwork` plus `LeaveTile`, shared by the top bar and the quick actions).
  - The animation respects reduced motion.
  - Help is updated (Getting started, Network, the + in WonderWorks modules).
  - E2E GJ-008 and GJ-008b open and fold the quick actions.
- **PR:** see the squash commit.
