## Desktop: no top bar; the avatar, name and bell at the foot of the sidebar

**What:**
- From 1024px the top bar is gone. The account controls render at the sidebar's foot (`TopBar place="side"`): the avatar with the name and career status, which opens the account menu (over the page, via a portal, so the sidebar's scroll doesn't clip it), and the bell with its count. ⌘K stays.
- Find's search row is back at the top of the page from 1024px, as one row with Refine just before Search. WJ-778's top-bar portal (`InTopBar`, `topFind.ts`) is removed.
- Career Profile's sticky bar sits at the top from 1024px (`lg:!top-0`).
- Phones and tablets are unchanged: the top bar keeps the module menu, the bell and the avatar.
- Help's "Getting around" text is updated.

**Why:** Owner, 2026-11-06: "remove the concept of top navbar completely, bring the avatar and notifications in sidebar bottom left, like implemented in WonderArk".

**Notes:** Tablets (768–1023px) keep the top bar, because their icon-only sidebar has no module list and the top bar's menu is how they switch modules. Only one of the two account controls renders at a time (`useMediaQuery`), so ⌘K and the admin check run once.
