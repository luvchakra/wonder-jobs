# WJ-866: Share a community: on the network, and on WhatsApp, X, Facebook and more

- **What:**
  - **Two buttons on every community** (beside its Owner, Moderator or Joined badge):
    - **Post to my network:** a post with the community attached as a card (name, "Community · Public · N members · N discussions", the start of its about), with the member's own words on top.
    - **Share:** the shared sheet with My network, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, X, email, Copy link and the phone's share sheet.
  - **Where the link goes:** a public community's link is `/c/<id>`, a page anyone can read: name, about, members and discussions, never who's in it, with Join the community. Signed-in visitors are redirected into the app. A private community's link is its in-app page, and `/c/<id>` shows nothing about a private one.
  - **Where the card can go:** feed posts and community discussions can carry a community (`social.ts::publishPost`, `domain/network/communities.ts::cleanAttached`). `preview.ts` renders its card, and tapping it opens the community in the app.
  - **Post sheet default:** sharing a community from its own page posts to the feed, not into the community itself.
- **Why:** Owner request (2026-11-23): "add options to share community on the app and social media options".
- **Notes:**
  - Files: `server/network/communities.ts::communityCard`, `server/network/preview.ts`, `server/network/social.ts`, `app/c/[id]/page.tsx`, the community page, `wonderworks/listing/ShareButton.tsx` (no summary fetch for non-listings; module from the page), `components/network/ListingCard.tsx` (no image row when there's no image), `PostSheet.tsx`.
  - Help is updated (Network).
- **Privacy:** the open-web page shows only what a public community already shows everyone on the network (name, about, counts) and is `noindex`. It never shows members, and never anything about a private community.
- **Tests:** `communities.test.ts`, "sharing a community": the public card, private stays hidden, posts and discussions carry a community, other app pages are refused. Checked at phone width: the share sheet, the post with the community card, the public page.
