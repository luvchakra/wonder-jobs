# WJ-835: Communities and discussions on My network

- **What:** My network gets **Communities**: a new shortcut beside Messages, plus Communities, community and discussion pages.
  - **Communities:** Yours first (each shows how many discussions have something new), then Discover and Search.
  - **New community** takes a name, what it's about, rules, who can read it (anyone on the network, or members only) and how people join (anyone, or moderators approve). Its creator becomes the owner.
  - **Roles:**
    - Owner: edits or deletes the community, and names moderators and owners.
    - Moderators: approve or decline requests, remove or ban members, and pin, lock or delete discussions and replies.
    - Members: start discussions, reply, like, follow, edit or delete their own, and report others'.
  - **Discussions** sort Active, New or Top, with pinned ones first. A dot marks a discussion with something new.
  - **Long discussions stay easy to follow:**
    - Replies nest under what they answer, with a guide line per level.
    - Tapping the picture on a reply that has answers folds that branch to "+ N more replies"; Collapse all / Expand all does every branch.
    - A discussion with more than 40 replies opens with its big side branches folded, never one that holds something new.
    - OP marks the discussion's starter, and New marks replies since your last visit; "Next new" jumps between them.
    - Past four levels, replies stop indenting and show "↪ Name".
    - Replies sort Oldest or Top.
    - A deleted reply leaves "This reply was deleted", so the replies under it still make sense. A blocked person's reply folds to a placeholder.
  - **Notifications:**
    - The person a reply answers, and everyone following the discussion, is told.
    - Starting or replying to a discussion follows it.
    - Moderators are told about join requests, and the person is told when approved.
- **Why:** Owner request (2026-11-22): "in my network, allow creation of communities and creation of discussion threads, keep it sleek and elegant, provide all features as present in typical social network ... discussion threads should be easy to follow even when the discussions are long and complicated".
- **Notes:**
  - Migration `0058_network_communities.sql` adds `network_communities`, `network_community_members`, `network_threads`, `network_replies`, `network_votes` and `network_thread_marks`. Until it is applied, the pages say "Communities aren't switched on yet".
  - Rules: `domain/network/communities.ts` (`layOut`, `MAX_DEPTH`, `can`). Server: `server/network/communities.ts`. Routes: `api/network/communities`, `communities/[id]`, `communities/[id]/members`, `discussions/[id]`. Pages: `app/app/network/communities/...`.
  - **Security / governance:**
    - Every action checks the caller's own membership and role on the server.
    - A private community's discussions and members are readable only by its members. A ban makes the community unavailable to that person.
    - Starting a community or a discussion, replying and liking go through `resolveCapability("network.post")`. They are audited without their words, as are moderator actions.
    - People reach the browser only as a `MemberView`.
    - Blocked people's discussions are hidden, and their replies are folded.
    - Reports go to the shared moderation queue.
  - **Account data:** new `ACCOUNT_DATA` entry `communities`.
    - Export: the person's memberships, discussions and replies.
    - Erasure:
      - an owned community passes to its longest-standing moderator or member, or is deleted when nobody is left;
      - the person's discussions go;
      - their replies elsewhere lose their words and author;
      - their likes, marks and memberships go.
    - Registered in the erasure coverage test.
  - **Tests:** `server/network/communities.test.ts`, `domain/network/communities.test.ts`, and the route journey's 401 list. Checked with a two-person phone-width browser walk.
