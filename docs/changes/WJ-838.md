# WJ-838: Deleting an account deletes what the person sent, with a full warning first

- **What:**
  - **Account deletion now removes what the person sent to others:**
    - applications sent with Easy Apply, with their résumé copy, answers, events and the company's notes on them;
    - requests sent through modules (internship applications, teaching demo requests, quote requests…), with their events and files;
    - demo bookings.
    A booking the person offered as a school member stays the other person's, naming a former member.
  - **Full warning:** the Delete account dialog now loads a preview in the person's own numbers.
    - Also deleted for good: applications and to how many companies, requests by kind, bookings, network connections and posts, and communities they own with nobody else in them.
    - Passed on without their name: hiring workspaces they own, either to the longest-standing member, or kept with open jobs closed when nobody else is in them; and communities they own.
    - If they sent anything, they must also tick "I understand … will no longer see them" before Delete everything works.
- **Why:** Owner decision (2026-11-22): option 1 ("Delete them"), "give full warning at the time of delete". The erasure coverage test (WJ-846) had pinned these as known gaps that contradicted the privacy notice ("applications … until you delete your account").
- **Notes:**
  - `server/sentAccount.ts` (`ACCOUNT_DATA` entry `sent`).
  - `server/privacy/erasePreview.ts` (`GET /api/privacy/erase`); the type is in `domain/privacy`.
  - `components/privacy/YourDataCard.tsx`.
  - `KNOWN_GAPS` in `erasure.coverage.test.ts` is now empty, and its end-to-end world expects no row to name the deleted person.
  - No migration: child rows cascade by foreign key and are also removed explicitly.
  - Tests: `server/privacy/erasePreview.test.ts` and the coverage test.
