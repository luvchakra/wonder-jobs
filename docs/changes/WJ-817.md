# WJ-817: People you've asked or connected with stay on Network's People tab

- **What:** With nothing typed, the People tab now also lists the people the member already stands with: requests waiting for them first (Respond), then suggestions, then people they asked (Requested) and their connections (Connected). The heading reads "People" when any of those show. Accepting, ignoring or withdrawing refreshes the tab.
- **Why:** Owner request (2026-11-21): "even after pending request, or accepted request the user should be visible under people tab". Before, a person vanished from People the moment a request was sent.
- **Notes:** `server/network/network.ts::people`, the card's Respond button, the page heading and help. Blocked people and ended or ignored requests the member received stay hidden, as before. Tests in `server/network/network.test.ts`.
- **PR:** see the squash commit.
