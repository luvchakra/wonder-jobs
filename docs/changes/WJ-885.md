# WJ-885 · Jobs · DONE

**What:** A typed or role search is answered only by a job that names all of its words. A job that names just one of them can no longer reach a high match.
- Every word must be in the job for more than a low fit (54).
- Every word must be in its title, tags, skills, requirements, company or place for more than a stretch (64). A word only mentioned in the description doesn't count.
- A job's perks no longer place it in a field. "Health insurance", "dental & vision insurance" and "insurance coverage/benefits/plans" are taken out of the description before it's searched.
- Insurance's own words count as "insurance": insurer, reinsurance, underwriting/underwriter, claims, actuarial/actuary, broking, insurtech, property and casualty, bancassurance, Lloyd's. Reinsurance's are reinsurer, retrocession, facultative and treaty underwriting.

**Why:** Owner, 2026-11-26: "check why it's not able to look for job in insurance domain". Searching as Reinsurance ("Insurance operations") ranked "Senior Specialist, TA Operations" (Delivery Hero) at 76 and "Sr. Manager of IT & Operations" (Osano) at 72. Two causes:
- The cap took any one search word in the title ("operations") as the field.
- "Health insurance" in a benefits list counted as the word "insurance".

**Notes:**
- `services/jobs/matching.ts::computeMatch` caps from `relevance()` for typed and role searches. `relevance.ts` adds `onlyInBody` and `withoutPerks`. Synonyms are in `normalize.ts::TERM_SYNONYMS`.
- Tests: `roleScope.test.ts` covers the owner's two jobs (now low fits), claims and insurer jobs leading, and insurance mentioned only in passing (stretch at most). `relevance.test.ts` covers perks. These tests fail on main and pass with this change.
- The AI relevance check (#804) runs only once the search has finished. The owner's screenshot was mid-search ("Matching to your profile…"), so the AI hadn't read those jobs yet.
- Profile searches (no words typed) are unchanged.
- No automation, AI or external-action change.
