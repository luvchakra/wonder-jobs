# WJ-834: Only me, and changing who sees a post after it's posted

- **What:**
  - New post has a third audience, **Only me** (kind `post:me`). Nobody else is ever shown it: not connections, not the feed, not reactions, comments or reports.
  - On your own post, the audience under your name (Connections / Everyone on the network / Only me) is now a menu. Pick another to change who sees it, at any time.
  - Reposts follow the change. A repost shows the original only to people who may still see it, and says it's no longer available to the rest. Repost is offered only while a post is for everyone.
- **Why:** Owner request (2026-11-22): "allow changing of visibility after a post is created, add only me option as well".
- **Notes:**
  - `server/network/social.ts`: `ONLY_ME`, `kindFor`, `setAudience`. The connections filter now applies in `feedFor`, `seeable` and `reportUpdate`.
  - Route: `POST /api/network/updates/[id]` with `{action: "audience"}`.
  - **Governance:** making a post visible to more people is posting again, so it goes through `resolveCapability("network.post")`; narrowing never needs it. The change is audited as `POST_AUDIENCE_CHANGED`. Only the author can change it.
  - No migration.
  - Tests: `server/network/engage.test.ts`.
- **PR:** see the squash commit.
