# WJ-559 · Jobs · DONE

**What:** Searching as a role (e.g. "Reinsurance", search terms "Insurance operations") now ranks jobs by how well they answer the role's own search terms and goal. A job that never mentions them is a low fit and sinks, instead of ranking first on My profile's IAM skills. The industry line on these jobs no longer claims "your target industry", because a role has no industries. The AI read of the leading jobs is told the role (its name, search words and goal), and each read is kept per role.
**Why:** Owner, 2026-11-25: "i don't think it's matching reinsurance properly, can you use ai to do the matching and finding the right results". Screenshot: searching as Reinsurance, the top card was "Director of Engineering, KYX" at 84.
**Notes:**
- Cause: a role's run never reached the role's words in scoring. `typedQuery` dropped them for role runs, so each posting was judged on the profile's headline ("IAM & AI"). The store's rescore treated the role's catalog as My profile's (`catalogKey` is "" for roles), and the AI read was sent `profileForAi(dna)`, i.e. My profile's goal. Fix: `services/jobs/roleScope.ts` (`searchWordsOf`, `scopeForJob`, `roleKey`), used by the browser and scheduled run scoring, the store's rescore (per job, via `foundAs`) and the AI read. `computeMatch` takes `forRole` so it doesn't weigh the profile's industries for a role.
- Before/after on the reported case (`roleScope.test.ts`, rules only, before the AI band): "Director of Engineering, KYX" went from 64 (stretch) to 54 (low fit); "Head of Reinsurance Operations" went from 50 (low fit) to 72 (worth considering).
- AI governance: no new model call, prompt or capability. The existing AI read (`/api/ai/rank`, `server/ai/rank.ts`) gets the role in place of the profile's goal in `roleWanted`. Its output is still only a bounded nudge to the rules' score (+15 / −20, `blendAiFit`), labelled "AI read of the posting". It runs once per search on the 30 leading jobs and is cached per job, keyed by profile and role. Posting text stays data. I didn't add a second AI relevance pass (a new capability): the existing one already does this job once it knows the role, and a second call per search would double the cost for the same answer.
- WJ-559 is the last id in Jobs' range (WJ-540–559). The next Jobs change needs a new block from the shared-layer session.
