# WJ-819 · WorksLake gateway · DONE

**What:** WonderArk can ask which companies are hiring for given words (`POST /v1/prospects/signals`, signal `hiring`). Each company comes back with how many of its recent Jobs postings matched and up to five of their titles, its open-job counts from the company directory, and Wikidata's industry, size band and head office. The source list offers it as "Companies hiring for…".
**Why:** Owner, 2026-11-11 (`docs/PROSPECT_DATA_API.md`, package A7): companies showing a buying signal, for offerings sold to companies that hire for a tool or a role.
**Notes:**
- Never returned: a posting's description, apply link, salary, its source, or anything about who posted it. Matching happens on the server; only titles and counts leave. Tests prove it at the hook and at the route.
- Only the newest 1,000 Jobs postings in the window (1 to 30 days) are read, and a company needs a domain a source reported or its own apply link shows. `metadata.postingsRead` says how many were read.
- Terms pending (owner decision `prospects-hiring-signals`): the titles come from sources reviewed for WonderWorks only, so every result says `termsPending: true`. Facts shown with a signal are Wikidata's only.
- A country is matched by its code or its full name; a bug where the code "IN" matched any place containing those letters was caught by the new test before it shipped.
- A signal request counts against a WonderArk business's free monthly allowance.
