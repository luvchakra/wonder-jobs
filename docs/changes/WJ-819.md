# WJ-819: Search speed-up, measured, and one more per-search cost removed

- **What:**
  - Records the before and after of WJ-816 (same request, fresh production build on the real project, measured by the E2E session through its proxy, source answering from cache):

    | Search | Before | After, run A | After, run B |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | First on a fresh server | 17.7 s | 9.3 s | 6.2 s |
    | Second | 2.9 s | 1.6 s | 1.1 s |
    | Third | 3.3 s | 2.0 s | 1.7 s |

  - Source health for planning is now kept per server instance for two minutes (`healthForPlanning`). Summarising a week of runs cost about 0.65 s on every search and 2.4 to 3.5 s on a cold one. A search that asks sources itself drops the copy, so a source that just failed is still held back at once on that instance.
  - Old cached answers are dropped at most once an hour per instance, not every 10 minutes (the delete took 5.8 to 8.7 s after the response).
- **Why:** After WJ-816, health plus keys was the slowest opening read on every search. Measured first, as the performance rules ask.
- **Notes:**
  - Left as measured: the warm pool load on a cold instance (2.9 to 3.5 s for 1,000 postings, 5.4 MB) and the search cache read (0.3 to 2.2 s per search).
  - Tests: `core.test.ts` checks a search with nothing new recorded plans without reading the runs again, and one after a live search reads them.
